## Creative Scenario

My Database can be used by Research Scientists for observing the different bird species, regions and characteristics,\
which can be used for studying the different types of species that exist in different parts of the world and all their\
qualities in order to better understand them. To do this, I will create 4 tables within the database "birds": "species",\
"characteristics", "country" and "region". The tables can then be linked together by their keys, with "region" acting as\
the link table, where Research Scientists can analyse the relationships between the tables, linking each bird species to\
their characteristics and countries they are from. In order to build meaningful interpretations, I will use aggregate\
functions and joins in order to get summary statistics (through aggregate functions) and connect the fields from the tables\
(through the use of joins like an inner join, for example). Finally, I will also provide a variety of demonstrations on\
the different ways of how a Research Scientist can work with the database.
